**1. Residents have the right to access their own medical records. If a resident asks to see their file, you should:**

a) Let them look through their file immediately
b) Notify your supervisor who will facilitate access to their records
c) Tell them they can't see their records until they're discharged
d) Only show them if they have a lawyer

**2. Which of the following is an example of respecting a resident's right to dignity?**

a) Discussing their incontinence issues in front of other residents
b) Calling an 85-year-old woman "a good girl" when she finishes lunch
c) Knocking before entering their room and addressing them by their preferred name
d) Making decisions for them without asking their input

**3. The facility administrator asks you to tell Mr. Davis he cannot have visitors on Saturdays anymore because "he gets too excited and it disrupts the routine." This would violate:**

a) Mr. Davis's right to receive visitors

c) Safety regulations
d) No rights - facilities can restrict visitors anytime
